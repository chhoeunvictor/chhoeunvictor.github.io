Overview

Troubleshooting Flowdesk

This guide helps you diagnose and resolve common issues in Flowdesk — a fictional collaborative workspace tool. Each section covers a specific problem: what you'll see, what's likely causing it, and how to fix it step by step.

Issues are organized by category. If you're not sure where to start, check Before you start for general diagnostic steps that resolve most problems.

Note

This is a portfolio writing sample by Victor Chhoeun. Flowdesk is a fictional product. All content is illustrative and written to demonstrate troubleshooting documentation structure and style.

Overview

Before you start

Run through these steps before diving into a specific issue. They resolve the majority of Flowdesk problems and take less than two minutes.

1
Refresh the page.

Most display and loading issues resolve with a hard refresh. Press Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac.

2
Check your internet connection.

Flowdesk requires a stable connection. Open another site to confirm you're online.

3
Check the Flowdesk status page.

If Flowdesk is experiencing an outage, the issue isn't on your end. Visit status.flowdesk.io to check current service health.

4
Clear your browser cache.

Stale cached data can cause unexpected behavior. Clear your cache in your browser settings, then reload Flowdesk.

5
Try a different browser or incognito window.

If the issue disappears, a browser extension is likely interfering. Disable extensions one by one to identify the cause.

Tip

Flowdesk works best in the latest version of Chrome, Firefox, or Edge. If you're on an older browser version, update it before troubleshooting further.

Connection & Authentication

Can't sign in

If you can't sign in to Flowdesk, use the steps below to identify and fix the issue. Most sign-in problems are caused by incorrect credentials, an expired account, or SSO configuration issues.

🔑
Incorrect email or password Most common ▼
Symptom

"Incorrect email or password" message appears after entering credentials.

Likely cause

Password entered incorrectly, or the account uses SSO and doesn't have a Flowdesk password.

Resolution

Reset your password or sign in via your SSO provider.

Steps to resolve
1
Check that Caps Lock is off and re-enter your password carefully.
2
Select Forgot password on the sign-in page. Enter your email address and follow the reset instructions sent to your inbox.
3
Check your spam folder if the reset email doesn't arrive within 5 minutes.
4
If your workspace uses SSO (Google, Okta, or Microsoft), select your SSO provider on the sign-in page instead of entering a password.
🚫
Account locked or deactivated Auth ▼
Symptom

"Account locked" or "Your account has been deactivated" message on sign-in.

Likely cause

Five or more failed sign-in attempts trigger a temporary lock. Admins can also manually deactivate accounts.

Resolution

Wait 30 minutes for a locked account to unlock, or contact your workspace admin to reactivate your account.

Steps to resolve
1
If locked: wait 30 minutes, then try signing in again.
2
If deactivated: contact your workspace admin and ask them to reactivate your account in Settings → Members.
3
If you are the admin and are locked out, contact Flowdesk Support with proof of ownership to regain access.
⚙️
SSO sign-in fails or loops Auth ▼
Symptom

Redirected to SSO provider but returned to sign-in page without signing in, or an error appears from the SSO provider.

Likely cause

Misconfigured SSO settings, email domain mismatch, or the SSO provider isn't returning the expected attributes.

Resolution

Verify SSO configuration in workspace settings or contact your IT administrator.

Steps to resolve
1
Confirm your email domain matches the domain configured for SSO in your workspace. Domains are set in Settings → Security → SSO.
2
Clear cookies and cached data for both Flowdesk and your SSO provider's domain, then try again.
3
Check the SSO error message from your provider — it usually contains a specific error code your IT admin can investigate.
4
Ask a workspace admin to verify that the SSO callback URL in your provider matches exactly: https://app.flowdesk.io/auth/sso/callback
Note

SSO issues require admin access to resolve. If you're not a workspace admin, share this article with your IT team.

Connection & Authentication

API key rejected

If your API requests return a 401 Unauthorized error, your API key is likely invalid, expired, or missing from the request.

🔐
401 Unauthorized on every request Most common ▼
Symptom

Every API request returns 401 with "unauthorized" or "invalid_key" in the error body.

Likely cause

The API key is missing from the header, formatted incorrectly, or has been revoked.

Resolution

Verify the key is present and correctly formatted in the Authorization header.

Steps to resolve
1
Check that your request includes the Authorization header with the correct format:
Header format
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY
2
Confirm you're using the correct key type. Test keys (fd_test_) only work against the sandbox environment. Live keys (fd_live_) are required for production.
3
Check that the key hasn't been revoked. Go to Settings → Developer → API keys and confirm your key shows as Active.
4
If the key is revoked or missing, generate a new one in Settings → Developer → API keys → Create key. Update your application with the new key.
Warning

Never paste your API key directly into code that will be committed to a public repository. Use environment variables to store keys securely.

⏱
Key worked before but now returns 401 Auth ▼
Symptom

API key was working, then started returning 401 without any changes on your end.

Likely cause

Key was manually revoked by a workspace admin, or the workspace plan was downgraded and API access was removed.

Resolution

Check the key's status and your workspace plan in Settings.

Steps to resolve
1
Go to Settings → Developer → API keys and check the status of your key. If it shows Revoked, create a new key.
2
Check your workspace plan. API access requires a Pro or Enterprise plan. Go to Settings → Billing to confirm your current plan.
3
Check the audit log in Settings → Security → Audit log to see if an admin recently revoked the key.
Connection & Authentication

Frequent sign-outs

If Flowdesk signs you out repeatedly or more often than expected, the steps below will help identify and fix the cause.

🔄
Signed out after a short time Most common ▼
Symptom

Returned to the sign-in screen after leaving Flowdesk idle, even for a short time.

Likely cause

Your workspace has a short session timeout configured, or your browser is set to clear cookies on close.

Resolution

Check workspace session settings, or adjust your browser's cookie behavior.

Steps to resolve
1
Check your browser's cookie settings. If your browser clears cookies on close, your Flowdesk session will end every time you close the browser window.
2
If you're a workspace admin, go to Settings → Security → Session timeout and increase the session duration.
3
Enable Stay signed in on the Flowdesk sign-in page. This extends your session to 30 days on trusted devices.
4
Avoid using incognito or private browsing. Session cookies don't persist between private windows, so you'll be signed out every time you close the window.
Performance

Pages load slowly

Slow page loads in Flowdesk are usually caused by a slow network connection, a very large page, or a temporary spike in service load. Use the steps below to identify and reduce the impact.

🐌
All pages load slowly Most common ▼
Symptom

Every page in Flowdesk takes several seconds to load or displays a spinning indicator for a long time.

Likely cause

Slow or unstable internet connection, or Flowdesk is experiencing elevated response times.

Resolution

Test your connection speed and check the Flowdesk status page.

Steps to resolve
1
Test your connection speed using a speed test tool. Flowdesk requires a minimum of 5 Mbps for a smooth experience.
2
Check status.flowdesk.io to see if Flowdesk is experiencing elevated response times or an ongoing incident.
3
Switch to a wired connection if possible. Wi-Fi interference can cause inconsistent speeds that affect real-time apps like Flowdesk.
4
Close unused browser tabs and extensions. Heavy browser usage reduces the memory available to Flowdesk.
📄
One specific page loads slowly Performance ▼
Symptom

One page is noticeably slower than others. Other pages in the same workspace load at normal speed.

Likely cause

The page contains a large amount of content, many embedded files, or a deeply nested sub-page structure.

Resolution

Reduce the page's content size by splitting it into sub-pages or removing unused embedded files.

Steps to resolve
1
Check how many embedded files the page contains. Each embedded image, video, or file adds to load time. Remove files that are no longer needed.
2
Split large pages into sub-pages. Pages with more than 5,000 words or 20+ embedded files benefit significantly from being broken up.
3
Remove deeply nested sub-pages. Flowdesk renders sub-page previews on parent pages. More than three levels of nesting can noticeably slow rendering.
Tip

Keep frequently accessed pages lean. Move reference material or archives to a dedicated section of your workspace to keep working pages fast.

Performance

Search is slow or missing results

🔍
Search results are incomplete or missing Most common ▼
Symptom

A page you know exists doesn't appear in search results, or results are significantly out of date.

Likely cause

The page was recently created or edited and the search index hasn't updated yet. New content can take up to 5 minutes to appear in search.

Resolution

Wait 5 minutes and search again. If the issue persists, check the page's permissions.

Steps to resolve
1
Wait 5 minutes after creating or editing a page, then search again. Search indexes update every few minutes.
2
Check that you have access to the page. Search only returns pages you have permission to view. If the page is restricted, it won't appear in your results even if it exists.
3
Check that the page isn't archived. Archived pages are excluded from search results by default. To include them, add archived:true to your search query.
4
Try a broader search term. Flowdesk search matches exact words and common variations, but doesn't support fuzzy matching. Use a shorter, more general keyword.
Performance

Editor is unresponsive

⌨️
Typing is delayed or the editor freezes Performance ▼
Symptom

There is a visible delay between typing and characters appearing, or the editor freezes entirely for a few seconds.

Likely cause

Low device memory, a very large page, or a browser extension interfering with the editor's event handling.

Resolution

Close background tabs, disable extensions, or split the page into smaller sub-pages.

Steps to resolve
1
Close unused browser tabs. Each open tab consumes memory. Closing tabs frees up resources for Flowdesk's editor.
2
Open Flowdesk in an incognito window to rule out browser extension interference. If the editor is responsive in incognito, disable extensions one by one to find the culprit.
3
Reload the page with Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R to clear any memory leaks from a long editing session.
4
If the page is very long, consider splitting it into sub-pages. Pages over 10,000 words can cause editor performance to degrade.
Permissions & Access

Can't access a page

If you receive a "You don't have access" message when opening a page, the page is either restricted to specific members, or you don't have the right role in the workspace.

🔒
"You don't have access" error on a page Most common ▼
Symptom

Opening a shared link shows "You don't have access to this page" even though you're signed in.

Likely cause

The page is restricted to specific members, or the sharing link has expired or been revoked.

Resolution

Ask the page owner to share the page directly with you, or grant you access from the page's share settings.

Steps to resolve
1
Confirm you're signed in to the correct account. If you have multiple Flowdesk accounts, you may be signed in to one that doesn't have access.
2
Ask the page owner to open the page, select Share, and add your email address with at least Viewer access.
3
If you should have workspace-wide access, ask a workspace admin to check your member role in Settings → Members and confirm it's set to Member or higher.
4
Check that the sharing link hasn't expired. Links set to expire automatically will stop working after the expiry date.
Permissions & Access

Can't share with a user

📤
User's email isn't found when sharing Most common ▼
Symptom

Typing a user's email in the share dialog shows "No user found" or the email doesn't autocomplete.

Likely cause

The user isn't a member of the workspace, or the workspace restricts sharing to specific email domains.

Resolution

Invite the user to the workspace first, or check whether domain restrictions are blocking the share.

Steps to resolve
1
Check that the email address is correct. A typo will prevent the user from being found.
2
Invite the user to the workspace before sharing. Go to Settings → Members → Invite member and send an invite to their email. Once they accept, you can share pages with them.
3
Check domain restrictions. If your workspace restricts sharing to approved domains, go to Settings → Security → Sharing and confirm the user's domain is on the allowlist.
Note

Only workspace admins can modify domain restrictions or invite members from outside an approved domain.

🚫
Share button is greyed out or missing Permissions ▼
Symptom

The Share button on a page is greyed out, disabled, or not visible at all.

Likely cause

You have Viewer access to the page, which doesn't include sharing permissions. Only Editors and Owners can share.

Resolution

Ask the page owner to upgrade your access level to Editor or Owner.

Steps to resolve
1
Check your access level by selecting the Share button. Your current permission level is shown next to your name.
2
Ask the page owner to change your access from Viewer to Editor or Owner from the Share dialog.
3
If you're a workspace admin and believe you should have full access, go to Settings → Security → Permissions and confirm that admin override is enabled for your workspace.
Permissions & Access

Member has wrong permissions

👤
A member can't do something their role should allow Most common ▼
Symptom

A member reports they can't perform an action — editing, deleting, or sharing — that their role should permit.

Likely cause

The member's workspace role is correct, but their access to the specific page is set to a lower permission level, overriding their workspace role.

Resolution

Check and update the member's page-level permissions in the Share dialog for that specific page.

Steps to resolve
1
Confirm the member's workspace role in Settings → Members. The role displayed there is their workspace-wide default.
2
Open the specific page they can't access and select Share. Find the member's name in the list and check their page-level access. Page-level settings override workspace role settings.
3
Update their page-level access to the appropriate level — Viewer, Editor, or Owner — depending on what they need to do.
4
If the issue affects many pages, consider updating their workspace role instead of adjusting individual page permissions. Workspace roles apply to all pages by default.
Tip

Page-level permissions always take precedence over workspace roles. If a member has an Editor workspace role but Viewer access on a specific page, they'll only be able to view that page.
